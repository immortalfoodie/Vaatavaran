US AQI VS INDIA NAQI

This app uses the US AQI scale (0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate, 101–150 Unhealthy for sensitive groups, 151–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very unhealthy, 301–500 Hazardous). India’s CPCB National AQI uses wider bands (e.g. 101–200 is labelled Moderate), so numbers and labels are not directly interchangeable.

STEP 01

NO-COST FIRST STEP

Remove the sources

Skip incense, candles, indoor smoking, and aerosol sprays. Damp-dust rather than stirring up settled dust.

STEP 02

IF BUDGET ALLOWS

Filter thoughtfully

Use a particle air cleaner sized for your room, following the manufacturer’s instructions. Avoid ozone-generating devices. Plants do not replace filtration.

STEP 03

CONTEXT MATTERS

Time your ventilation

Keep windows closed during outdoor pollution peaks if safe. Ventilate when outdoor air improves. Do not ignore heat, combustion fumes, or other indoor hazards.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Data you can trace

When configured, live mode uses the Google Air Quality API (500 m sensor-fused grid, converted to US EPA AQI, cached 10 minutes), with optional WAQI ground-station support. Regional forecasts and 12-hour outlooks come from Open-Meteo and Copernicus CAMS (cached 10 minutes). Demo mode uses fictional, fixed scenarios for ten Indian cities. Forecasts can change.

One scale, clearly labeled

All categories use US AQI: 0–50 good, 51–100 moderate, 101–150 unhealthy for sensitive groups, 151–200 unhealthy, 201–300 very unhealthy, 301–500 hazardous. This differs from India’s National AQI. Do not compare category labels across scales.

Advice with boundaries

Our rule engine prioritizes exposure, household sensitivity, commute, budget and purifier access. Source-control and filtration advice is informed by EPA guidance and AirNow. Smoke guidance supports particle-reduction practices; it does not cover every urban gas pollutant.

Privacy & generative AI

Settings and daily completion are stored in your browser. Chat is kept only in page memory. With AI enabled and your opt-in, your question, selected city, air data and household preferences are sent through our server to Google Gemini. Free-tier content may be used by Google to improve its products. Do not include identifying or medical details. No API key reaches the browser. AI can be wrong; the rule-based plan remains independently available.

Deployment limits

The public demo can run without any AI key. An Internet deployment with a shared AI key should add user authentication, HTTPS and an external rate limiter to protect the free quota. This app’s in-memory limits reset on restart.
