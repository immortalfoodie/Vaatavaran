YOUR CLEANER-AIR CORNER

Start with the room you use most.

01

Remove the sources

Skip incense, candles, indoor smoking, and aerosol sprays. Damp-dust rather than stirring up settled dust.

No-cost first step
02

Filter thoughtfully

Use a particle air cleaner sized for your room, following the manufacturer’s instructions. Avoid ozone-generating devices. Plants do not replace filtration.

If your budget allows
03

Time your ventilation

Keep windows closed during outdoor pollution peaks if safe. Ventilate when outdoor air improves. Do not ignore heat, combustion fumes, or other indoor hazards.

Context matters
Outdoor data cannot tell us your indoor AQI. These are source-control practices, not a claim that your home is safe. See EPA’s filtration guidance ↗.

A clear view of our assumptions.

Data you can trace

Demo mode uses fictional, fixed scenarios for six Indian cities. Live mode requests modelled air quality from Open-Meteo and Copernicus CAMS, cached for 10 minutes. This is a model estimate, not a street-level sensor reading. Forecasts can change.

One scale, clearly labeled

All categories use US AQI: 0–50 good, 51–100 moderate, 101–150 unhealthy for sensitive groups, 151–200 unhealthy, 201–300 very unhealthy, 301–500 hazardous. This differs from India’s National AQI. Do not compare category labels across scales.

Advice with boundaries

Our rule engine prioritizes exposure, household sensitivity, commute, budget and purifier access. Source-control and filtration advice is informed by EPA guidance and AirNow. Smoke guidance supports particle-reduction practices; it does not cover every urban gas pollutant.

Privacy & generative AI

Settings and daily completion are stored in your browser. Chat is kept only in page memory. With AI enabled and your opt-in, your question, selected city, air data and household preferences are sent through our server to OpenAI. Do not include identifying or medical details. No API key reaches the browser. AI can be wrong; the rule-based plan remains independently available.

Deployment limits

The public demo can run without any AI key. An Internet deployment with paid AI should add user authentication, HTTPS, provider spending caps and an external rate limiter. This app’s in-memory limits reset on restart.
